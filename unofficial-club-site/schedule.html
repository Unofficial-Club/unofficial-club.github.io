Want the floor?

If you've got 5 to 15 minutes of enthusiasm about something — a proof, a failure mode, a paper that changed how you think, a machine you built — that's a talk.

Want to present? Just show up. Want to listen? Just show up — the door's open either way. There's no lineup to check ahead of time: who ends up talking, and in what order, isn't decided until the session actually starts.
